---
name: Supporter post privacy and community tags
description: Owner-only private posts and the difference between a recommendation tag and community membership.
---

Private supporter posts are owner-only, **not** visible to approved followers.
Changing a supporter profile to private must also hide its existing posts and
protect direct photo/video requests, not merely remove cards from the feed.

**Why:** The user explicitly specified that private-profile posts show only to
their author, unlike the follower-visible private accounts on some social sites.

**How to apply:** Preserve this rule across profiles, Following, For you,
thumbnails and playback. A follow never grants access to private posts.

A community tag is a recommendation signal, not membership or an access rule.
Do not automatically join groups or infer diaspora identity from a tag or a
country association.

**Why:** The requested composer has one optional audience tag; the product has
no separate community-membership system. Tagging a community can address it
without asserting that the author belongs to it.

**How to apply:** Personalise public recommendations using actual interests,
follows and explicit tag choices. Keep these signals separate from privacy and
from any future membership feature.
