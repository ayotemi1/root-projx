---
name: Onboarding audience model
description: The distinct acquisition and access model for supporters and organisation representatives.
---

Supporters create an account immediately and continue into discovery and giving. Organisation representatives submit an application, wait for review, and receive platform access only after approval.

**Why:** Combining both audiences in one registration flow created contradictory expectations, duplicated organisation details, and made the two journeys feel interchangeable.

**How to apply:** Route supporter acquisition to immediate registration and organisation acquisition to the review-based application. Keep public calls to action, login redirects, confirmation copy, and future onboarding changes aligned with this distinction.
