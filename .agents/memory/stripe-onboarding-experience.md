---
name: Stripe onboarding experience
description: Keep the organisation dashboard open during external Stripe setup.
---

Stripe setup should open in a separate tab, not replace the organisation
dashboard.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a separate page after confirming
onboarding was working, so organisations retain their place in the dashboard.

**How to apply:** Preserve that behaviour for both starting and continuing setup.
An expired-link refresh should remain in the already-open setup tab rather than
opening another tab.
