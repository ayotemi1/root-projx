---
name: Organisation content
description: Public profiles should show organisations' work through browsable photos, videos and optional social links.
---

Organisation profiles need a visible library of content documenting their work, including watchable videos, photos and optional Instagram links. Connecting an Instagram account must not be required.

Treat this as general **posts**, not an updates-only workflow. Each organisation
has an Instagram-style grid, and the Live Feed includes every kind of post from
organisations. Photos/videos can be uploaded directly from a device; a progress
update, category, title or caption must not be required for a media post.

**Why:** The user wants visitors to see enough real content to understand what an organisation has done and to give its profile life beyond fundraising information. They explicitly corrected the earlier updates-oriented implementation: this should be any kind of post.

**How to apply:** Keep on-site uploads and public social links usable independently. Do not promise that an Instagram link alone can always be played inline; uploaded video is the reliable on-site viewing option.
