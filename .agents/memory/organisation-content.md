---
name: Organisation content
description: Public profiles should show organisations' work through browsable photos, videos and optional social links.
---

Organisation profiles need a visible library of content documenting their work, including watchable videos, photos and optional Instagram links. Connecting an Instagram account must not be required.

**Why:** The user wants visitors to see enough real content to understand what an organisation has done and to give its profile life beyond fundraising information.

**How to apply:** Keep on-site uploads and public social links usable independently. Do not promise that an Instagram link alone can always be played inline; uploaded video is the reliable on-site viewing option.
