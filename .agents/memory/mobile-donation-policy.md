---
name: Mobile donation policy
description: Why mobile donations must be processed on the Root ProjX website, not through native payment sheets.
---

Mobile donations must happen on the website, not within the native app. Use the website hand-off to rootprojx.com rather than collecting donation details or payments through a native payment sheet.

**Why:** The user reported that Apple rejected the iOS app under guideline 3.2.2(iv) for collecting donations in-app and explicitly required website-only donations.

**How to apply:** Preserve this restriction when changing mobile donation entry points, campaigns, organisation screens, and payment integrations. Do not reintroduce native donation collection.
