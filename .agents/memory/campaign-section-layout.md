---
name: Campaign section layout
description: Shared presentation rule for campaign story and box sections
---
All campaign sections with a headline and copy use a left-aligned two-column desktop header: about 55% headline and 45% copy, separated by 56px, with their bottom edges aligned. The first paragraph is the lead; remaining paragraphs stay in the same copy column. On phones, headline and copy stack at full width. Box groups fill every row: one full-width, two halves, three thirds, four as two pairs, five as three then two, six as two rows of three, and larger groups as rows of three with the final row stretched.

**Why:** The user supplied a common Out of Many Hands reference and asked for Drum Life and every other campaign to stop using jarring full-width/centred headings and partial box rows.

**How to apply:** Keep campaign-specific colours, fonts, copy, donation interface, funding bar and interactive wall/drums intact. Use the shared section arrangement in both editor preview and public campaigns rather than maintaining bespoke story or budget layouts.
