1. Who We Are
Root ProjX Ltd is a private limited company incorporated 
in England and Wales. We operate the Root ProjX platform 
— a community giving platform connecting diaspora supporters 
to verified grassroots organisations worldwide.
Registered address: 51 Russell Row, Lewes, BN7 2EE
Contact: info@rootprojx.com | www.rootprojx.com
Root ProjX Ltd is registered with the ICO as a data 
controller under UK GDPR.

2. What Data We Collect
Account data: full name, email, phone (optional), 
password (encrypted), account type.
Organisation data: org name, contact name, country, 
registration number (optional). Bank details are 
collected by Stripe only — not Root ProjX.
Donation data: amount, recipient org, timestamp, 
donor email. Payment details stored by Stripe only.
Usage data: pages visited, orgs followed, search 
queries, device type, IP address.
Communications: newsletter subscriptions, support 
messages.

3. How We Use Your Data
To create and manage your account. To process donations 
and send receipts. To connect supporters with 
organisations. To send newsletters and impact updates 
(opt-in only). To improve the platform. To comply with 
legal obligations. To detect and prevent fraud.
We do not sell your data. We do not use your data 
for advertising.

4. Legal Basis for Processing
Contract, legitimate interests, consent (newsletters), 
legal obligation.

5. Data Storage & Security
Data stored on Google Firebase (Google Cloud EU/UK 
servers, UK GDPR compliant). Payments handled by 
Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1). We never store card numbers 
or bank credentials.

6. Data Retention
Account data: active period plus 2 years after closure.
Donation records: 7 years (UK financial regulations).
Usage data: 12 months then anonymised.
Newsletters: until you unsubscribe.

7. Your Rights
Access, rectification, erasure, restrict processing, 
data portability, object to processing, withdraw consent.
Contact info@rootprojx.com to exercise any right. 
We respond within 30 days.

8. Age Policy
13+ to use the platform. 18+ to donate independently.
13-17 year olds may donate with parental consent.
Under 13s: contact info@rootprojx.com to remove account.

9. Cookies
Essential cookies for authentication and sessions. 
Analytics cookies to understand platform usage. 
Control via browser settings.

10. Third Party Services
Stripe (payments), Google Firebase (storage), 
Brevo (email), Apple App Store / Google Play (distribution).

11. Changes to This Policy
Material changes notified by email. Latest version 
always at www.rootprojx.com/privacy.

12. Contact & Complaints
Email: info@rootprojx.com
ICO complaints: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113

1. About Root ProjX
Root ProjX Ltd is a private limited company (51 Russell 
Row, Lewes, BN7 2EE) operating a community giving platform. 
We are not a registered charity. Organisations on the 
platform are independently verified but not required to 
be registered charities.

2. How Donations Work
95% of every donation goes directly to the organisation.
5% is retained by Root ProjX as a platform fee.
Payments processed securely by Stripe.
Root ProjX does not hold donor funds — all funds flow 
directly to organisations via Stripe Connect.

3. Platform Fee Disclosure
Display this as a clean table:
£10.00 donated → £9.50 to org / £0.50 to Root ProjX
£25.00 donated → £23.75 to org / £1.25 to Root ProjX
£100.00 donated → £95.00 to org / £5.00 to Root ProjX

4. Organisation Verification
Organisations verified before receiving donations via 
document review, contact confirmation, and Stripe 
identity verification. Verification is not endorsement.

5. Refund Policy
All donations are final and non-refundable.
Discretionary refunds considered only for: technical 
duplicate charges, fraud, or org account suspension.
Request within 48 hours at info@rootprojx.com.

6. Age Requirements
18+ to donate independently. 13-17 with parental consent.

7. Gift Aid
Not currently available. We intend to apply for Gift Aid 
intermediary status and will notify donors when available.

8. Organisation Responsibilities
Use funds for stated purposes, post regular updates, 
comply with local laws, notify Root ProjX of any changes, 
complete Stripe Connect onboarding.

9. Limitation of Liability
Root ProjX is a technology platform only. Not responsible 
for how organisations use funds or accuracy of their 
profile information. Total liability capped at the 
donation amount.

10. Governing Law
England and Wales.
