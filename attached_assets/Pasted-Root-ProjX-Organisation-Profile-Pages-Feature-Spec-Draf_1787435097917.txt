Root ProjX — Organisation Profile Pages
Feature Spec (Draft for Temi)
 
1. Purpose
The org profile page is the trust anchor of the platform. It needs to make verification, transparency, and impact tangible for a supporter deciding whether to give — and it should feel like a living relationship, not a static charity brochure.
 
2. Core Identity & Credibility
• Org name, logo, location (parish/community), founding year
• Verification badge — visible indicator of what's been checked (registration status, references, site visit, etc.)
• Mission statement in the org's own words, not a Root ProjX paraphrase
• Photos/video of actual work — no stock imagery
 
3. Transparency Layer
• Funds received to date + broad usage categories (operations, materials, staff, etc.) — doesn't need line-item detail but should feel concrete
• Recent updates feed — short posts with photos ("here's what your support funded this month")
• Named contact person/leader at the org, for accountability and human connection
 
4. Giving Mechanics
• One-off vs recurring donation options
• Specific funding "asks" tied to a concrete goal (e.g. "roof repair — $2,000 of $5,000 raised") rather than a generic donate button
• Supporter wall — opt-in names only, no amounts shown
 
5. Social / Network Layer
• "X of your friends support this org" signal
• Group/circle giving call-to-action on specific funding asks
• "Notify a friend" button on the page
 
6. Trust & Risk Flags
• Last updated date — staleness itself is a transparency signal (e.g. no posts in 6 months should be visible to supporters)
• Any pending flags/reviews surfaced by the moderation process
 
7. Story & Context
• Origin story — why the org exists, what problem it solves
• Connection to broader context (e.g. Hurricane Melissa recovery), to help donors feel part of something bigger than one org
 
8. Data Ownership: Org-Editable vs Root ProjX-Curated
Key open decision for Visaka + Temi: how much of the page content is self-reported by the org vs verified/curated by Root ProjX.

9. Open Decisions for Visaka + Temi
• How much of the page is org-editable vs Root ProjX-curated (see section 8)
• What the audit/spot-check cadence for funds usage reporting looks like
• How verification badge tiers are defined and displayed (single badge vs tiered levels)
• Whether funding asks require Root ProjX approval before going live
